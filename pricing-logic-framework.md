# Pricing Logic Framework
## Internal Reference

### Overview

MaisieCompany pricing is structured around recurring coordination effort required to manage compliance obligations. Pricing reflects the operational workload of scheduling, tracking, documenting, and coordinating activities across buildings, systems, and contractors.

### Pricing Drivers

Pricing is determined by factors that directly correlate with coordination effort:

**Number of Buildings**
- Each building requires separate obligation mapping, contractor coordination, and documentation management
- Multiple buildings increase tracking complexity and coordination overhead

**Number of Systems/Assets**
- Each regulated system (e.g., fire alarm, sprinkler, lifts, pressure systems) requires separate obligation tracking
- More systems mean more schedules to manage, more contractors to coordinate, and more documentation to collect

**Contractor Count**
- Each accredited contractor relationship requires separate coordination
- Higher contractor count increases communication overhead, scheduling complexity, and follow-up requirements

**Frequency of Statutory Obligations**
- Services with more frequent obligations (e.g., monthly testing vs annual inspection) require more active coordination
- Higher frequency obligations generate more scheduling, documentation collection, and ongoing oversight

**Combined Complexity Factors**
- Geographic spread of buildings
- System interdependencies requiring coordinated scheduling
- Multiple stakeholder contacts per building

### What Pricing Does Not Depend On

Pricing does not vary based on:

- Inspection results or pass/fail outcomes
- Risk assessment findings or identified deficiencies
- Remediation requirements or corrective actions
- Historical compliance performance
- Building age, condition, or complexity beyond system count
- Regulatory penalty history or enforcement actions
- Client insurance premiums or coverage levels
- Audit outcomes or findings

Coordination effort remains constant regardless of whether an inspection passes or fails, or whether remediation is required. MaisieCompany coordinates the obligation process—the outcomes are determined by accredited third-party providers and regulatory bodies.

### Pricing Structure Principles

- **Transparent**: Pricing drivers can be explained clearly to clients
- **Defensible**: Pricing reflects actual coordination workload
- **Scalable**: Pricing model accommodates portfolio growth
- **Neutral**: Pricing does not create incentives around outcomes or results

### Client Discussion Framework

When discussing pricing with clients:

1. Emphasize coordination effort, not compliance outcomes
2. Quantify drivers: number of buildings, systems, contractors, obligation frequency
3. Clarify that pricing covers coordination and documentation, not inspection/testing work itself
4. Explain that pricing remains consistent regardless of inspection results or remediation needs
5. Frame pricing around administrative burden reduction and operational consistency
