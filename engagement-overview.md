# Engagement Overview
## Client-Facing Draft

### What MaisieCompany Does

MaisieCompany provides managed coordination services for infrastructure-critical building systems. We take responsibility for the scheduling, tracking, and documentation that keeps your compliance obligations on track across multiple buildings and systems.

**Coordination Activities:**
- Mapping your statutory and regulatory obligations across buildings and systems
- Scheduling inspections and tests according to compliance requirements
- Coordinating with accredited contractors to confirm attendance and completion
- Collecting certificates, reports, and documentation from contractors
- Maintaining consolidated compliance records accessible when needed
- Providing status updates on obligations and outstanding items

### What MaisieCompany Does Not Do

**MaisieCompany does not:**
- Carry out regulated inspections, testing, or certifications
- Provide regulatory advice or legal interpretation
- Assume legal responsibility for compliance outcomes
- Perform physical work on building systems
- Conduct risk assessments or remediation work
- Guarantee inspection pass rates or compliance outcomes
- Act as a signatory authority for compliance certificates

All inspection, testing, and certification work is performed by accredited third-party providers who hold the relevant qualifications and regulatory approvals. We coordinate and document—we do not certify.

### Legal Responsibility

Legal responsibility for compliance remains with the property owner or designated responsible person. MaisieCompany operates as a coordination service provider, not as a compliance authority or regulatory agent. We coordinate the process; the legal and regulatory obligations belong to you.

### How Coordination Works in Practice

**Intake**
Initial conversation to confirm suitability and understand your building portfolio, existing obligations, and current contractor relationships. We complete an intake form capturing building details, systems, and existing compliance obligations.

**Obligation Mapping**
We map your statutory and regulatory obligations across all buildings and systems. This includes identifying required inspections, testing frequencies, contractor requirements, and documentation needs. You review and confirm the obligation map before coordination begins.

**Ongoing Coordination**
We manage the day-to-day coordination work: scheduling inspections according to requirements, confirming appointments with accredited contractors, following up on completed work, collecting certificates and reports, and updating your compliance records. We handle routine coordination so you don't have to.

**Reporting and Documentation**
You have access to your consolidated compliance records through MaisieSystem at any time. We provide regular status updates on obligations due, completed, and outstanding. Documentation is stored centrally and is audit-ready when insurers, auditors, or regulators require it.

### Typical Engagement Flow

1. **Initial Conversation**: We discuss your building portfolio, compliance obligations, and current approach. We confirm whether our coordination service is a fit.

2. **Scope Confirmation**: We define the buildings, systems, and obligations to be covered. Monthly retainer is confirmed based on coordination effort required.

3. **Intake**: We complete building and system details, map existing obligations, and identify accredited contractors already in use.

4. **Obligation Mapping**: We build a complete picture of required obligations, schedules, and contractors. You review and approve the obligation map.

5. **Coordination Service Begins**: We take over day-to-day coordination: scheduling, contractor liaison, documentation collection, and record maintenance.

6. **Ongoing Service**: Month after month, we handle coordination work, maintain records, and keep you informed of status. You focus on higher-value priorities.

### Ongoing Relationship

This is an ongoing service relationship, not a one-time project. We maintain coordination month after month, building after building, so you have consistent oversight without the administrative burden. If obligations change, buildings are added, or contractors are replaced, we update the coordination accordingly.

### Questions or Clarifications

If you have questions about what coordination includes, what remains your responsibility, or how the engagement would work for your portfolio, we're available to discuss details before you proceed.
