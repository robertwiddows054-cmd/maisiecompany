# Service Delivery Standard Operating Procedure
## High-Level Internal SOP

### Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for delivering coordination services consistently across all client engagements. It establishes boundaries, responsibilities, and escalation paths to ensure operational consistency and service quality.

### Scope

This SOP applies to all coordination services delivered through MaisieSystem, including but not limited to Fire & Life Safety Management and Statutory Inspection & Testing Coordination. The process is designed to be extensible to additional service types without rewriting.

---

## 1. Client Onboarding

### 1.1 Initial Qualification
- Confirm client manages multiple buildings or sites with recurring compliance obligations
- Verify client has designated responsible person with legal authority for compliance decisions
- Assess suitability: coordination service fit, not seeking inspection/certification provider

### 1.2 Scope Definition
- Document buildings to be covered (addresses, property types, occupancy details)
- Identify systems/obligations to be coordinated (e.g., fire safety, statutory inspections)
- Confirm existing contractor relationships or identify need for contractor sourcing
- Note any special requirements or constraints (e.g., restricted access times, key stakeholders)

### 1.3 Engagement Confirmation
- Confirm monthly retainer based on pricing logic framework
- Agree on communication preferences and reporting frequency
- Establish primary client contact and escalation contacts
- Confirm service start date and any transition period requirements

### Boundaries
- Do not take on buildings or obligations outside agreed scope without formal scope change
- Do not assume legal responsibility for compliance outcomes
- Do not provide regulatory advice or interpretation

---

## 2. Obligation Identification

### 2.1 Obligation Mapping
- Research applicable statutory and regulatory requirements for each building and system
- Document required inspections, tests, certifications, and frequencies
- Identify required contractor qualifications and accreditation requirements
- Map interdependencies (e.g., inspections that must be coordinated together)
- Build obligation schedule with due dates, frequencies, and contractor requirements

### 2.2 Client Review
- Present complete obligation map to client for review
- Client confirms obligations are correct, frequencies are accurate, contractors are appropriate
- Client signs off on obligation map before coordination begins
- Document any client-requested exclusions or modifications with rationale

### 2.3 Record Creation
- Create obligation records in MaisieSystem for all confirmed obligations
- Link obligations to buildings, systems, contractors, and schedules
- Set up automated reminders and tracking based on obligation frequencies

### Escalation Points
- If statutory requirements are unclear or ambiguous, escalate to client for legal/regulatory interpretation—do not interpret requirements independently
- If client disputes obligation map, pause coordination until resolution
- If new obligations discovered post-implementation, document as scope change and seek client approval

---

## 3. Contractor Coordination

### 3.1 Contractor Identification
- Use client's existing accredited contractors where possible and approved
- If contractors must be sourced, identify accredited providers meeting qualification requirements
- Client must approve all contractors before coordination begins
- Maintain contractor contact information, accreditation details, and preferred communication methods

### 3.2 Scheduling
- Schedule inspections/tests according to obligation due dates and frequencies
- Confirm contractor availability and preferred scheduling windows
- Coordinate access arrangements with client or building management
- Send appointment confirmations to contractors, clients, and relevant stakeholders
- Follow up 48 hours before appointments to confirm attendance

### 3.3 Follow-Up and Completion
- Confirm contractor attendance and work completion
- Request certificates, reports, and documentation within agreed timeframes (typically 5 business days post-completion)
- Follow up on outstanding documentation if not received within timeframe
- Log completion in MaisieSystem and update obligation status

### Boundaries
- Do not instruct contractors on inspection methodology or testing procedures
- Do not approve or reject inspection results—results are between contractor and client
- Do not negotiate contractor pricing or contract terms—client manages commercial relationships
- Do not direct contractors to perform work outside agreed scope

### Escalation Points
- If contractor fails to attend scheduled appointment, notify client immediately
- If contractor provides non-compliant or incomplete documentation, escalate to client for resolution
- If contractor disputes scheduling or coordination, involve client in resolution
- If contractor repeatedly fails to meet obligations, recommend client consider contractor replacement

---

## 4. Documentation Handling

### 4.1 Document Collection
- Collect certificates, inspection reports, test results, and compliance documentation from contractors
- Verify documents are complete, signed by accredited provider, and dated appropriately
- Request corrected or missing documentation if initial submission is incomplete
- Log document receipt in MaisieSystem with receipt date and document type

### 4.2 Document Storage
- Store all documentation in MaisieSystem with appropriate metadata (building, system, obligation type, date, contractor)
- Ensure documents are accessible to client through MaisieSystem portal
- Maintain document retention according to agreed retention periods
- Maintain document security and access controls

### 4.3 Document Quality Checks
- Verify documents are from accredited providers
- Confirm documents match scheduled obligation (correct system, correct type, correct date)
- Flag any discrepancies (e.g., failed inspection, outstanding actions) to client immediately
- Do not alter, modify, or redact documentation

### Boundaries
- Do not authenticate or verify document authenticity beyond confirming accredited provider
- Do not interpret inspection results or test findings—present findings to client as received
- Do not create compliance certificates or documents—only store and organize documentation provided by accredited contractors

### Escalation Points
- If contractor provides incomplete documentation, escalate to client with specific gaps identified
- If document raises compliance concerns (e.g., failed inspection, safety issue), notify client immediately
- If document authenticity is questioned, escalate to client and contractor for resolution

---

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring and Reporting

### 5.1 Obligation Status Tracking
- Monitor obligation due dates and upcoming deadlines
- Track completion status and outstanding items
- Identify obligations approaching due dates and confirm scheduling is in place
- Flag overdue obligations immediately to client

### 5.2 Status Reporting
- Provide regular status updates to client (frequency per agreement, typically monthly)
- Include: obligations due, scheduled, completed, overdue, and upcoming
- Highlight any concerns or items requiring client attention
- Maintain reporting frequency as agreed, avoiding unnecessary noise

### 5.3 Client Notification Triggers

**Immediate Notification Required:**
- Contractor fails to attend scheduled appointment
- Inspection/test results indicate compliance failure or safety concern
- Obligation becomes overdue without scheduled completion
- Documentation received is incomplete or non-compliant
- Contractor reports issue requiring client decision (e.g., system access denied, equipment fault)

**Regular Notification (Per Reporting Schedule):**
- Monthly status summary of obligations due, completed, outstanding
- Upcoming obligations in next reporting period
- Any pattern concerns (e.g., recurring contractor issues, documentation delays)

**Do Not Notify:**
- Routine scheduling confirmations (unless client requests)
- Normal documentation collection process (unless delayed)
- Standard coordination activity that is proceeding as expected

### Boundaries
- Do not create alarm where none exists—differentiate between routine coordination and genuine concerns
- Do not guarantee compliance outcomes in reporting
- Do not provide regulatory interpretation or advice in status reports

---

## 6. Service Boundaries and Responsibilities

### 6.1 MaisieCompany Responsibilities
- Coordinate scheduling of obligations according to confirmed obligation map
- Liaise with approved accredited contractors for scheduling and documentation
- Collect and organize compliance documentation
- Maintain consolidated compliance records accessible to client
- Provide status reporting on obligations and coordination activity
- Escalate issues requiring client attention or decision

### 6.2 Client Responsibilities
- Provide accurate building and system information during intake
- Confirm obligation map is correct before coordination begins
- Approve contractors before coordination begins
- Manage commercial relationships with contractors (pricing, contracts, disputes)
- Make decisions on compliance issues, remediation, or regulatory matters
- Maintain legal responsibility for compliance outcomes
- Provide access to buildings and systems for scheduled work
- Review and approve any scope changes or additional obligations

### 6.3 Third-Party Contractor Responsibilities
- Perform inspections, testing, and certification work according to accreditation and regulatory requirements
- Provide accurate, complete, signed documentation within agreed timeframes
- Attend scheduled appointments or provide adequate notice of changes
- Communicate technical findings directly to client when required

---

## 7. Escalation and Issue Resolution

### 7.1 Issue Categories

**Coordination Issues:**
- Scheduling conflicts, contractor attendance problems, documentation delays
- Resolution: Standard follow-up, client notification if persistent, potential contractor replacement recommendation

**Compliance Issues:**
- Failed inspections, outstanding actions, regulatory concerns
- Resolution: Immediate client notification, client determines response and remediation

**Scope Issues:**
- New obligations identified, building changes, system modifications
- Resolution: Document as scope change, seek client approval, adjust obligation map accordingly

**Service Quality Issues:**
- Client complaints, repeated coordination failures, communication breakdowns
- Resolution: Internal review, process improvement, potential service termination if unresolved

### 7.2 Escalation Path
1. **Standard Coordination**: Handle through standard SOP processes
2. **Client Notification**: Escalate to client for decision or awareness per notification triggers
3. **Internal Review**: Escalate to service lead for process review or service quality assessment
4. **Engagement Review**: Escalate to engagement lead for scope or contractual discussion

---

## 8. Service Termination and Transition

### 8.1 Service Termination
- Client may terminate service with agreed notice period
- MaisieCompany may terminate service if client responsibilities are not met or scope boundaries are repeatedly violated
- Provide complete documentation package to client upon termination
- Transfer obligation records and historical documentation to client format

### 8.2 Service Transition
- Document current obligation status, scheduled work, and outstanding items
- Provide client with contractor contact information and obligation schedules
- Ensure continuity of critical obligations during transition period if agreed
- Complete documentation handover per agreed format

---

## 9. Continuous Improvement

### 9.1 Process Refinement
- Review coordination efficiency and identify process improvements
- Gather client feedback on service delivery and adjust accordingly
- Document lessons learned and update SOP as needed

### 9.2 Service Extensibility
- This SOP structure is designed to accommodate additional service types (e.g., HVAC coordination, environmental compliance)
- When adding new services, map new service activities to existing SOP sections
- Add service-specific requirements as subsections without rewriting core process
- Maintain consistent boundaries, escalation paths, and client notification triggers across all services
